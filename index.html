Not just the code.

A plausible answer can still be wrong. I look for evidence that the system preserves meaning, handles awkward inputs, and gives people something they can inspect.

  • Make assumptions, sources, and limits visible.
  • Design for missing evidence and recoverable failures.

01 / CONVERSATIONAL INTELLIGENCEPILOT
eMEANING + CONTEXT + PROOF

Echo From report hunting to trusted answers.

An AI-ready reporting intelligence framework that makes governed analytics conversational without dropping context, control, or evidence.

Semantic intelligencePower BIEvaluation
Open the product notebook +

The friction

The answer can already exist in a report, but finding it requires knowing its pages, measures, filters, and analytical conventions. A fluent AI answer is not enough if it loses those rules.

The product direction

Connect semantic intelligence (what the data means), report intelligence (how it is used), and evaluation intelligence (how behavior is verified). Interpret the question, resolve ambiguity, plan, execute read-only, and return evidence.

Engineering boundaries

The model interprets; analytical engines calculate. Source permissions remain authoritative. Answers should disclose their measure, period, filters, source, and limitations. Ambiguity should prompt clarification, not guesswork.

Where it stands

The current product overview describes a working pilot with role-based briefings, analytical follow-ups, read-only execution, and Golden Question evaluation. Broader business launch remains a separate adoption and governance step.

My focus

Connecting conversational interaction, analytical context, and evaluation into a coherent product direction. This case study highlights the system design and engineering decisions, not a public product demonstration.

The decision that matters

Keep interpretation separate from calculation. An answer needs its meaning and evidence, not just fluent language.

02 / MODEL ASSURANCECONCEPT
SCANASSUREFIX ↗
AI PROPOSES. HUMANS APPROVE.

Lumo Make the change. Keep the meaning.

A proposed model-improvement system that separates discovery, deterministic assurance, and approved, reversible remediation.

Model evidenceQuality gatesHuman approval
Open the product notebook +

The friction

A model change can look reasonable while breaking a report or altering business meaning. Missing evidence must not be mistaken for proof that a change is safe.

The proposed system

Scan inventories evidence and runs deterministic rules without mutation. Assure captures baselines, regression results, and benchmarks. Fix prepares a reviewable candidate diff, classifies risk, and requires approval.

The execution boundary

Candidate changes belong in development. Tests and human approval precede controlled CI/CD deployment. A findings store, audit trail, and rollback support accountability.

Where it stands

Lumo is a target architecture and product exploration, not a claimed production deployment. Its proposed evidence sources include model definitions, metadata, query baselines, and optional usage telemetry.

My focus

Shaping the product concept and proposed architecture: evidence collection, assurance, reviewable changes, and controlled execution. Implementation and production outcomes are not claimed here.

The decision that matters

A finding is not permission to change a model. Missing usage evidence should hold a removal candidate, not justify it.

SKU rationalization Similar isn't always equivalent.

A Python pipeline for hierarchical BOM traversal, specification-aware similarity clustering, and explainable consolidation review.

PythonGraph traversalAutomated QA
Open the engineering notebook +

The problem

Product records can look alike but differ in specifications or their place in a bill of materials. Consolidation decisions need more than text similarity.

My contribution

Built the SKU rationalization pipeline: hierarchical BOM graph traversal, feature normalization, specification-aware similarity clustering, explainable Excel outputs, and automated QA checks.

End-to-end shape

Source records → relationship traversal → normalized features → similarity groups → explainable review output → quality checks. The output supports human consolidation decisions rather than silently merging records.

What matters

A useful recommendation needs a reason a reviewer can inspect. This case is presented as a technical summary; client data, implementation details, and unverified outcome figures are not published.
